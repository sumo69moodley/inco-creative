Project description

iNCO Creative had the privilege of collaborating with ARTsolar, a leading name in the renewable energy sector, to create a dynamic and informative online presence. Our goal was to design a website that not only reflects ARTsolar's commitment to sustainable energy solutions but also enhances user engagement and accessibility.

What we did

  • Website design
  • E-commerce development
  • Content strategy & SEO copy
  • Product catalogue
  • Inquiry forms
  • Testing & launch

Our approach

How we built it.

01

Understanding ARTsolar's vision

Our initial phase involved in-depth consultations with ARTsolar's team to understand their brand, objectives and target audience.

02

Design and development

We crafted a user-friendly interface with a modern aesthetic that aligns with ARTsolar's brand identity. Key features included responsive design, intuitive navigation and interactive elements to engage visitors.

03

Content strategy

Our content team developed clear, concise and SEO-optimised content that effectively communicates ARTsolar's mission, services and impact in the renewable energy sector.

04

Technical implementation

We integrated advanced features such as a product catalogue, customer inquiry forms and a resource centre to provide comprehensive information to ARTsolar's clients and partners.

05

Testing and launch

Rigorous testing was conducted to ensure a seamless user experience across all devices. The website was then successfully launched, marking a new digital era for ARTsolar.

Project outcomes

What changed.

  • Enhanced online presence for ARTsolar, reflecting their industry leadership
  • Improved user engagement and accessibility
  • Positive feedback from ARTsolar's team and their clients on the website's usability and design
