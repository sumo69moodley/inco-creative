Company: Keep Group (Pty) Ltd t/a iNCO CreativeLaw: Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

This processing policy applies to the personal information (the “Content”) processed by KEEP GROUP (PTY) LTD (hereinafter referred to as “INCO CREATIVE”). The customers, clients and employees of INCO CREATIVE are referred to in this processing policy as our “Stakeholders”.

This Policy is in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

1. About this data processing policy

This Processing Policy applies to the personal information (the “Content”) processed by INCO CREATIVE to:

  • add new customers / suppliers / service providers / employees (hereinafter referred to as stakeholders) onto our system;
  • personal information collected by our Human Resources Department in the recruitment / employment / payroll process;
  • set up and / or implement Information Technology systems / solutions, as per our client's requirements and specifications.

2. Why we collect personal information

We collect Content in order to efficiently operate our business and:

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • Invoice customers
  • Pay suppliers and service providers
  • Perform pre-employment checks during our recruitment process
  • Pay Employees
  • Host our client's email servers
  • Host our client's website servers
  • Design and develop websites for clients, as per client's instructions
  • Brand, advertise and manage client's social media platforms
  • Create an identifiable and unique brand for each client
  • Produce videos that are used for client's advertising and / or work purposes
  • Attending on creating back-up systems of our client's business data
  • For marketing purposes

We do use the Content for research and analytical purposes to improve the Services we offer to our customers as detailed above.

6. Security

We have appropriate physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard and help prevent unauthorised access and maintain data security of, and to use correctly, the information we collect from you. These safeguards vary based on the sensitivity of the information that we collect and store. We have implemented procedures designed to limit the dissemination of your information to only such designated staff as are reasonably necessary to carry out the stated purposes we have communicated to you.

7. Retention of information

We will only keep your data as long as it remains necessary for the identified purpose(s) for which it was originally collected, in accordance with this Policy and our Data Retention Policy.
